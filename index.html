About Luca's Pokémon Centre

A Pokémon Trading Card Game collection and deck-building companion.

Where does the card information come from?

This site retrieves card details, expansion information, artwork and available price data from the public Pokémon TCG API. Card prices are not guaranteed to be live, complete or available for every printing. Prices are estimates, and USD prices are converted to GBP using an adjustable approximate exchange rate.

Price availability depends on the data supplied for each card. Clicking a price-source link opens the provider's card listing when one is available.

Your data

Your deck lists and collection are stored in your browser using local storage. They are not automatically synced to another device.
