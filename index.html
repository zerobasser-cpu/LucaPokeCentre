How to play

1. Start a battle and receive your opening hand.
2. Play a Pokémon from your hand if you need one.
3. Attach Energy once per turn.
4. Attack the opposing active Pokémon.
5. Knock out Pokémon to collect prizes. The first side to claim all prizes wins.

Tip: You can draw cards and attach Energy once per turn. If your active Pokémon is knocked out, promote a benched Pokémon.
